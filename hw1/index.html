Introduction

Image credit: https://medium.com/%40sanjeevanibhandari3/dark-patterns-are-still-everywhere-heres-what-i-found-in-10-popular-websites-0ee7fd3e5fa1

Have you ever attempted to cancel a subscription and found it difficult or frustrating? This is a common example of a dark pattern in UX design.

Dark patterns in UX design are deceptive or manipulative techniques used by websites or companies to subtly influence users into performing actions they might not otherwise take.

These techniques tend to exploit psychological principles and tendencies to encourage behaviors that will often be detrimental to the user, but beneficial to the company.

Dark patterns can be difficult to detect because they rely on subtle design elements that take advantage of user's expectations, habits, or experiences with standard UX design.
