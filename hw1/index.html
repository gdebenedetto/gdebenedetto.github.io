Dark Patterns in UX Design

Introduction Examples Impact Ethical UX

Introduction

Have you ever attempted to cancel a subscription and found it difficult or frustrating? This is a common example of a dark pattern in UX design.

Dark patterns in UX design are techniques used to manipulate users into performing actions they might not otherwise take.

They typically tend to benefit the company or developer at the expense of the user.
